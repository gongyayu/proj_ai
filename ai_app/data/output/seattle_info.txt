Seattle is the largest city in Washington state, known for its iconic Space Needle, vibrant coffee culture, and thriving tech industry. Nestled between Puget Sound and Lake Washington, it boasts a mild climate with abundant rainfall, lush green parks like Discovery Park, and scenic waterfronts such as Pike Place Market. The city is also home to major companies like Amazon and Microsoft, making it a hub for innovation and entrepreneurship. Seattle's diverse neighborhoods offer a mix of historic charm, modern architecture, and cultural attractions, including the Museum of Pop Culture and the Chihuly Garden and Glass exhibit.
